### Success Metrics

**Data Scientist:** Daniel Rodriguez III

Definitions:

-   **ARPPU (Average Revenue Per Paying User):** Total revenue from paying users / Number of paying users.
    *   *Relevance:* Direct measure of monetization efficiency and value extracted from the paying user base.

-   **Match→Message Rate:** (Number of matches resulting in at least one message sent) / (Total number of matches).
    *   *Relevance:* Key indicator of initial engagement quality and user intent post-match, crucial for assessing the effectiveness of the 'Dates' experience and matching algorithms.

-   **Offline Progression Efficiency:** A composite metric reflecting the rate at which matches progress to real-world dates or significant off-platform interactions. This could be proxied by:
    *   High message exchange volume post-match.
    *   Positive sentiment in messages.
    *   Exchange of contact information (if detectable).
    *   User-reported successful dates (if a survey mechanism is in place).
    *   *Relevance:* Directly measures the success of the platform in fostering meaningful connections, a core tenet of Bumble's 'quality reset'.
